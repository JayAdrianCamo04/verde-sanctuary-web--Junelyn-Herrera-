About Verde Sanctuary

Tourism that benefits visitors and destinations

Verde Sanctuary was created as a proposed tourism concept based on the idea that tourism should benefit both visitors and destinations.

The concern⚠️

Many people travel to coastal areas to relax, take photographs, and enjoy outdoor activities. However, tourism can also create waste, disturb natural habitats, and place pressure on local communities when it is not properly managed.

→
Our response🌿

Verde Sanctuary responds to this concern by combining recreation with education and environmental responsibility. It presents a tourism model where visitors are not only guests but also temporary partners in protecting the destination.

Visitor Guide

How to prepare for your visit

Verde Sanctuary is a working coastal learning site. A little preparation makes your visit safer, more comfortable, and kinder to the reef.

🎒

Bring

Tick items off as you pack
👕

Recommended clothing

What to wear and what to skip

Wear

  • Comfortable lightweight clothing
  • Proper footwear for outdoor activities
  • Quick-drying clothes for water activities
  • Hat or cap
  • Modest clothing when visiting local communities

Avoid

  • Slippery footwear during outdoor activities
  • Clothing that may restrict movement
  • Excessive jewelry
  • Items that may easily fall into the water
⛈️

Weather and activity changes

Conditions come first

Coastal activities depend on weather, sea conditions, and safety considerations. Verde Sanctuary may modify, postpone, or cancel an activity when conditions are unsafe.

Visitor safety and environmental protection take priority over completing an itinerary.
♿

Accessibility note

Visitors who require accessibility support should send an inquiry before booking. The availability of assistance may depend on the activity, location, weather conditions, and available facilities.

Send an inquiry →
🌿

Environmental reminders

Visitors should:
♻️Avoid single-use plastics
🧴Bring reusable containers
🪸Never touch or collect marine life
🐠Never feed wildlife
👣Avoid stepping on sensitive habitats
🧭Stay within designated areas
🤫Keep noise levels low
🗑️Take all personal waste with them
🗣️Follow the guide’s instructions
🦺

Safety reminders

Follow these every time
  1. Attend the safety briefing before every activity.
  2. Wear the required safety equipment.
  3. Stay with your group.
  4. Inform the guide about allergies, medical concerns, or physical limitations.
  5. Do not enter the water during unsafe conditions.
  6. Follow weather and sea-condition updates.
  7. Ask for assistance when needed.
  8. Keep emergency contact information accessible.
🤝

Suggested visitor etiquette

Be a respectful guest
  • Speak respectfully with local residents and staff.
  • Ask permission before photographing people.
  • Support local products responsibly.
  • Respect community spaces.
  • Avoid loud behavior in quiet areas.
  • Do not assume that every natural area is open for public access.
  • Follow local customs and regulations.
